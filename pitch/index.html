Cullyard

Tell your agent how to sort. Watch the folders move.

A local MCP server and desk that lets Claude Code or Antigravity sort and edit photos, video, music and documents, with each run sealed on Solana devnet.

github.com/bryankwandou/cullyard01

The problem

Every shoot ends in a folder of hundreds of files and no order.

Photographers

Culling takes hours

Picking keepers from a wedding or a product shoot is slow, repetitive work done one frame at a time.

Editors

Footage piles up

Clips, music beds and client PDFs land in one Downloads folder. Finding the right take means opening each one.

AI agents

They can't see the work

An agent can move files with shell commands, but you can't watch it, undo it, or show the client what happened.

Cullyard02

The product

  • Drop files onto a desk in the browser at 127.0.0.1
  • Your agent reads them over MCP and sorts into lanes
  • Cards move live as each tool runs
  • Disagree? Drag it back or press 1 to 9
  • Trim and resize write copies and never touch the original
Cullyard03

How it works

Eleven MCP tools. One folder. No upload.

StepToolWhat happens
Readscan_workspace · describe_fileKind, size, pixel size, duration, codec, artist, page count
Sortcreate_lane · move_to_laneMoves into lane folders, refuses to overwrite or leave the workspace
Editresize_image · trim_mediaWebP, JPG, PNG, AVIF copies; time-range cuts without re-encoding
Provelog_digest · seal_run · verify_sealSHA-256 of the move log, written to a devnet memo and checked back
Cullyard04

Why a chain at all

A receipt that the sort happened, and when.

A photographer hands over selects. A studio hands over a cut. The client can check that the log of moves was not edited later. Only a hash goes on-chain, never file names or files.
